## Design Context

### Users

Janus is an operations console for the people running an LLM gateway. They configure providers, credentials, public model routes, and fleet nodes; investigate usage, failures, logs, and audit history; and manage their own sign-in security. The interface must support confident decisions during both routine configuration and incident response.

### Brand Personality

Precise, calm, and dependable. The console should feel like a refined utilitarian instrument: quiet during normal operation, explicit about state, and loud only when the underlying system requires attention. Avoid marketing-style decoration, playful copy, and ambiguous status visuals.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the existing Geist and shadcn/ui system with a cool-tinted neutral palette, one restrained accent, compact cards, tabular numeric data, and accessible light and dark themes. Favor clear hierarchy and efficient scanning over visual spectacle. Do not introduce gradients as decoration, oversized dashboard hero metrics, excessive pills, or feature-by-feature visual redesigns.

### Design Principles

- Preserve existing workflows and make state, next actions, and destructive consequences unambiguous.
- Establish consistency through shared tokens and components before changing individual pages.
- Keep desktop information density high; adapt mobile layouts instead of hiding functionality.
- Use sentence case, direct operational copy, visible focus states, and motion that respects reduced-motion preferences.
- Treat loading, empty, error, stale, and partial-data states as first-class interface states.
