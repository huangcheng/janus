**Verdict: GO WITH FIXES** — internal math contradictions, undefined mid-stream chunked behavior, and provider-specific code-extraction paths need pinning before Part C ships.

**Critical risks**
1. **Probe economics undercount at scale.** DashScope bootstrap of 5 keys × 262 listings = 1,310 calls = 26% of `JANUS_PROBE_DAILY_CALLS=5000`. Two simultaneous bootstraps breach the ≥25% healing reservation; input_shape's 1h re-probe compounds. Alphabet tail-cut risks probing away Router-bound listings.
2. **First-call-vs-budget contradicts ceiling math.** "First call may exceed the budget" (60s TTFB + 30s body = 90s) yet the ceiling assumes retries fire (`budget + TTFB + body-cap + jitter`). If the first attempt eats the budget, `now < deadline` is false and no retries run — ceiling formula is unreachable in the case it bounds.
3. **Chunked mid-stream stall unaddressed.** "Committed at first stream_body byte" is correct, but `transfer-encoding: chunked` lacks body-length; cowboy's idle_timeout may not catch silence after first chunk, leaving open-then-503 streams retry-ambiguous.
4. **Balance-key thundering herd at TTL expiry.** Balance TTL 1h, healing loop 15min → up to 15 min of failing-key traffic before heal. No pool-level breaker; fleet-wide degraded upstreams hammer.
5. **Rotation fail-open window unbounded.** Secret update clears rows; traffic hits new secret blind until probe completes. With 150ms pacing × 262 listings × N keys, large pools stay fail-open for many minutes.

**Contradictions/stale claims**
- "Key calls ≤ max_attempts + 2" — first + retries + pool_reuse + provider retry = `max_attempts + 3`.
- `broken` TTL 24h but healed at ≥22h — margin unexplained.
- janus-auto target WITH failover runs 45s+ atop judge budget; client-budget arithmetic missing.
- "cooldown-EXPIRED, least-recently-used attempted key" mid-request is undefined: min cooldown 10s > typical retry interval, so no key can expire mid-loop.
