# Audit — target.md

**1. Verdict: GO WITH FIXES** — the design is coherent and failure-mode-driven, but contains one internal contradiction, one precedence hole that can disable the motivating failover case, and two claims that don't survive arithmetic.

**2. Critical risks**
- *False input_shape = terminal.* Misclassifying a deny-shaped 400 as input_shape (regex misses new wording) yields ZERO retries — worse than today's blind LB, which eventually cools the key. The spec optimizes against false `deny` but false `input_shape` is the silent killer.
- *Probe wall time.* Per-key sync probe "bounded ~2–4 min": 262 listings × 150 ms spacing + 15 s timeout per failing call ≫ 4 min; the 10-min cap truncates. Claim is stale.
- *Retry storms.* Damping relies on per-key cooldowns; the codes-empty 429 path contradicts itself on whether cooldown exists (see §3).
- *Failover vs idle_timeout:* ceiling math (180+60+30+30=300s) checks out; real exposure is client timeouts < 45 s budget — acknowledged, unmitigated.
- Streaming boundary, rotation staleness, balance starvation: adequately handled.

**3. Contradictions**
- Codes-table-empty 429: "classifies rate (retryable, **no cooldown**)" vs exhaustion contract "minimum 10 s cooldown even in the fail-open default."
- Part B fail-open "pick IGNORES the matrix" vs A.2 "broken keys are **never** fail-open candidates" — which wins when the only non-cooling keys are deny-marked AND the pool contains broken marks?
- Part B retains a self-Q&A artifact ("Both dialect variants ship as gateway migrations? No —").

**4. Missing requirements**
- Premarked modality listings: a deny-shaped body error on a premarked listing is TERMINAL by the premark rule — no precedence stated for body deny evidence overriding premark.
- No audit event for `entitlement_codes` admin writes (probes are audited; rule-table edits are the more dangerous operation).
- No guidance/override for per-route budgets where clients have 30 s timeouts.

**5. Architecture notes**
- Snapshot threading through attempts, binary listing-name deny keys, first-call-unconditional accounting, `is_terminal`-only billing with crash backfill linked to the ceiling: all sound.
- Pool-reuse reserved slot is mostly dead code: balance cooldown 60 s > default budget 45 s, so "cooldown-EXPIRED, LRU" rarely finds a candidate in-budget.
- Codes-table single-sourcing across probe/refresher/gateway is the strongest part; keep the seed as migration-only.

**6. Top 5 edits**
1. Pick one codes-empty 429 rule: min 10 s cooldown always.
2. Fail-open pick: explicitly exclude `broken` rows; all-broken pool → terminal with broken evidence.
3. Retry-time precedence: codes-table deny match in the body overrides premark/input_shape terminality.
4. Recompute per-key probe bound (spacing × listings + timeouts); make per-key probes async or cap listings per sync call.
5. Cap the reuse-slot cooldown at min(key cooldown, budget/2), or drop pool reuse from v1.
