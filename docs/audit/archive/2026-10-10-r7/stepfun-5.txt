## Residuals

**R1 (must-fix) — Part 0.2 target rule contradicts itself.** "with NO UNEXPIRED PASSIVE ROW (single freshness predicate…)" vs. "freshness counts BOTH row sources — … (passive OR probe)". An implementer reading the lead clause ships the rev-6 behavior (cap-exhaustion re-probing) that rev 7 explicitly closed. Delete "PASSIVE" and the "single" gloss.

**R2 (must-fix) — JSON contract gap.** Metrics pin `sched_probe_skip_total{reason=cap|cadence|fresh|drained}` (Part 0.2, Part C) but the pinned `/stats/sched` surface lists `"probe_skip_total": {"cap", "cadence", "fresh"}` only. Add `"drained": int` — the eunit addition list asserts a reason the assertion surface can't show.

**R3 (must-fix) — master-side recognition of internal completions is unpinned.** Seams 1–2 (skip `rtt_ms` at ingest; skip TTFB health feed) and clearing the in-flight probe map all require the master to know a given done/error is a probe. The spec pins only the worker-side journey ("the `internal` flag rides the job map to the worker"). Pin the mechanism: JobRef lookup in the pool's `{Node => JobRef}` in-flight map, or an additive `internal` key on done/error (compatible with the additive-keys envelope). Eunit 6(b) can't be written against an unnamed seam.

**R4 (must-fix) — re-hello trigger after adopt is missing.** Rev 7 makes adopt prune ALL `sched_workers` rows, but nothing states what causes each idle worker to re-hello — the new owner has no records to ask from. Without a pinned trigger (worker-side periodic hello, or hello-on-send-failure), the fleet stays empty until workers restart, defeating the "dist connections survive" claim.

**R5 (must-fix) — probe issuance trigger unnamed.** `dispatch_probe/2` consumes a pool-internal `{sched, 2, {probe, JobSpec}}`, but no timer/owner is pinned as its sender (presumably the 30 s tick). Gate 13.4b depends on it; name it.

**R6 (should-fix) — 13.3b/13.6 attribution vs. unlabeled metrics.** `sched_dispatch_worker_total{node}` has no provider label, so "us 0 for that provider's jobs" (13.3b) and "the fast worker wins on the unpinned job" (13.6) require single-provider job-stream isolation that the steps don't pin. Worse for 13.6: **any** unpinned fast job landing on the victim during the poisoning window dilutes its EWMA — two ~300 ms samples after three 8 s ones read ≈ 4631 < the 5000 floor ⇒ demotion never fires. Pin: quiesce all traffic except the two pinned providers during the window (the relational assertion fails loud, but the step would be flaky-by-design without this).

**R7–R12 (pin hygiene):**
- **R7:** Eunit 6 says clamp-down > 600000 is "bounded by `sched_rtt_dropped_total`" — contradicts Part 0.3/rev 6 (clamp-down is *not* a drop; only negative/non-finite increment the counter).
- **R8:** `SampledAtMono` is never defined as ingest-time vs. measurement-time; latest-wins and the 64-entry cap ordering depend on it.
- **R9:** "≤ 1 probe in flight per worker" is not wired into the target-rule eligibility — a second probe can be issued to a worker with one in flight. Exclude in-flight workers (skip reason `cadence`?) or state it.
- **R10:** Post-adopt, `sched_rtt` rows survive (only `sched_workers`/`sched_reserve` are pruned) while health-EWMA cold-resets — up to 10 min of pre-restart RTT alongside fresh health state. Document or prune.
- **R11:** "NORMAL pinned job" — the word "pinned" reintroduces affinity-pin ambiguity; the bypass sentence saves it, but reword.
- **R12:** Minor: "dropped+counted" for old-master `{sched, 2, _}` casts names no counter; 13.4/13.5 drain-to-0 assertions lack the bounded wait 13.2 pins; "concurrent piggyback-vs-cold-start race" is really out-of-order arrival (both writers are the pool gen_server — single-writer serialization already closes concurrency).

## Bottom line

No architecture or math defects; no prior-round regressions. Ratblock on **R1–R6** (two are spec-internal contradictions/gaps, four are unpinned seams that gate steps and mixed-restart behavior depend on), then the `[ ] pi-audit GO` box in Part G can be checked. R7–R12 are a wording/pin pass.
