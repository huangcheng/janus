**1. Verdict**
GO WITH FIXES — the contracts and phasing discipline are sound, but one real Phase-2 ordering contradiction, unvalidated error-frame shapes, and two coverage gaps must be fixed first.

**2. Critical risks**
- *Error-frame realism vs C6:* →chat failure emits `data:{"error":...}` and zero-frame failures flush a skeleton; neither is capturable from a happy-path corpus nor among the two allowed synthetic exceptions (1.0). OpenAI SDKs may not parse a mid-stream error chunk. SDK-validate or promote to documented exceptions.
- 1.0 mandates real `response.failed`/`response.incomplete` captures, but providers don't emit those deterministically; capture feasibility is unaddressed.
- Interleaving extends 4MiB deferred-text buffering onto previously zero-copy →chat/→anthropic paths; 50×4MiB=200MiB and the TTFT p95<10% check land only in 3.4 (last), so latency regressions surface last.
- Four index-space maps + full-accumulate-emit-at-close add per-frame overhead to every translated stream; no cost budget for native-pair passthrough.
