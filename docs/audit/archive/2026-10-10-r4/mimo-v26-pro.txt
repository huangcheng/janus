**2. Critical risks**
- **Probe/counter contradiction.** Part 0.6/B/C say affinity-pinned picks "increment but are never rolled back," yet probes "ride the affinity-pin path" AND "never touch the reserve counter (no increment, no decrement)". No seam is named (e.g., pick opt `reserve => false` threaded from the probe issuer). As written, the two rules conflict at the same code point.
- **`sched_rtt` row shape can't implement cold-start-only.** `{Node, ProviderId, RttMs, ExpiresAtMono}` has no source field, so "probe overwrites only when no passive sample exists; passive always overwrites" is undecidable. Add a source tag or a separate probe-fill key, or drop the rule.
- **Ack-miss reconcile ambiguity.** The reconcile "decrements the excess only" against `JobRef => Node`. If ack-missed jobs enter that tracked set, a silently dropped job leaks its slot until nodedown (the 30 s reconcile never settles it); if not, the reconcile over-releases. Pin membership + a max pending age.
