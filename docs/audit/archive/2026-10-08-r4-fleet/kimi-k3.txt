**1. Verdict: GO WITH FIXES** — round-2 folds are materially present, but the erpc exception contract is wrong as written and two rev-10 "applied" claims are contradicted by the body text.

**2. Critical risks (new parts)**
- **erpc error shape is mis-specified.** `erpc:call/5` raises `error:{erpc,timeout}` / `error:{erpc,noconnection}` — the documented wrapper `catch C:R -> {error,C,R}` yields `{error, error, {erpc, timeout}}`, never `{error, timeout}`. F.7's paused-peer assertion (`{error, timeout}`) and the "common classes: undef/noconnection/timeout" line will fail exactly as pinned. (`undef` does arrive as `error:undef` — that one is right.)
- **cache_put replay bound is leaky.** `expires_at ≤ first_seen + 2×TTL` doesn't say *which* TTL: a replay carrying a larger `TTLms` extends the entry past two original lifetimes. Bind must be `first_seen + 2×TTL_from_first_message`, and the `first_seen` table's own GC is unspecified (unbounded growth across Hashes).
- **`function_exported` precheck is local and lies twice:** (a) module not yet loaded on a fresh node → false negative; (b) says nothing about the peer (version skew is only caught by remote `undef` — which is fine, but then the precheck buys little and costs the false-negative case).
- **Quota mirror cap ambiguity:** rev 10 header says aggregate 8 192/sender across ALL mirrors; Part B and B2 bodies each say "row cap 8 192/sender" per table. Enforcement differs 3×.
- F.6/F.7 gate is testable; F.7's paused-peer slot is the one assertion that will red against real erpc until the shape is fixed.
