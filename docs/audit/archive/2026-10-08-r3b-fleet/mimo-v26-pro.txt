**1. Verdict: GO WITH FIXES** — rev 9's folds landed, but Part B2 leaves its two payload hooks unnamed and its replay bound unenforced, and F.6/F.7 assert state and timing the spec never defines.

**2. Critical risks**
- **Quota heartbeat storm**: "2 s coalesced heartbeat per key ≥50%" is still one message per key per tick — 500 hot keys ≈ 250 msgs/s/node over WAN. The row cap bounds storage, not message rate. Coalesce into one per-sender batched message per tick; cap keys per batch.
- **cache_put replay bound is asserted, not specified**: "extends life by at most one TTL" has no ingress rule (e.g. ignore cache_put for an unexpired Hash, or cap new ExpiresAt at old+TTL). As written, replay extends indefinitely.
- **cache_put writes violate the stated architecture boundary** ("everything lands in local ETS mirror tables"): receivers write *into janus-auto's working cache* — bypassing mirrors, the tolerant-read helpers, and local-wins. Name the ownership/sweeper path or mirror+consult.
- **Unnamed hook points**: B2 never states where quota counters are read (Slice-Q enforcement site?) or where cache_put is published (cache-fill path in `janus_auto`?) — Part B verified its three hooks against `janus_lb.erl`; B2 must do the same.
- **Fan-out params**: `lb_cool_clear {target}` and command-name parsing need `binary_to_existing_atom` + target ∈ known targets, checked locally before erpc — else atom-table growth and arbitrary-atom fan-out reopen the enum.
- **F.7 lease timing vs F.4**: `docker pause` does not fire nodedown until ~net_ticktime (60 s) — F.4 says so explicitly. The gate must wait ~65–70 s and say so; lease has no fencing (fine for observability-only, mandatory before phase-4 sweep duty).

**3. Contradictions / stale claims**
- Part C's `/stats.fleet` schema has no quota-mirror field, yet F.6 asserts `/stats.fleet` quota rows exist.
- Part 0.2 "local evidence wins" is undefined for cache entries injected remotely (a cached decision isn't evidence — say so).
- F.6(b) "byte-identical control run" needs fresh mock counters or delta comparison; cumulative counters make it vacuous.

**4. Missing first-ship requirements**
- Quota mirror in `/stats.fleet` + its metrics/sweep accounting.
- Audit-ring durability statement (in-memory, restart-lossy).
- `fleet_status` must be parked-safe (`janus_fleet` may be down).
