**2. Critical risks**

- **p95 still diverges across dialects**: A5's SQLite offset `trunc(Count*0.95)-1` ≠ nearest-rank `ceil(0.95N)-1`, which is what `percentile_disc(0.95)` returns. N=21: 19th vs 20th value. Use `ceil`.

- **Buffer failure**: A3 drops failed chunks into `dropped` but `dropped` is never exposed or flushed as a metric; also `build_insert` accumulates `SqlAcc ++ [..]` (quadratic) — fine at 50 rows, just sloppy.
