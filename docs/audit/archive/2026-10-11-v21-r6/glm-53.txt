- Admission primitive is correct: the atomic `ets:update_counter/4` with insert-default IS the decision; rollback-on-over cannot race; transient −1s are tick-healed; zero-row deletion correctly scoped to nodes absent from pool records.
- Nodedown select+take with take-owned decrement + insert-default provably closes double-decrement (concurrent release finds nothing; 128-bit JobRef collision ≈ 0).
- Drain-idle arming (tick + touched-poke; draining ∧ non-internal-empty ∧ reserve==0) cannot false-arm off a reconcile heal; the lost-probe stall is correctly narrowed to display-only lingering.
- Unary fallback: single process-dictionary predicate, chat-family-only, {'DOWN'}-only, release-first — sound; unprocessed-chunks-in-mailbox is subsumed by the named worker-buffered window.
