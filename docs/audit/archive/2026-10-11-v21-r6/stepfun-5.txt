## 1. Verdict
**GO WITH FIXES** — all seven rev-3.3 folds are present, coherent, and header/body-consistent (admission primitive, insert-failure heal, C.1 enumeration incl. worker rendering, DISPLAY-ONLY rtt_ms_avg, ALLOW_DIRTY, <20 s, buffered-bytes risk); residuals are implementability pins, not design defects.

## 2. Critical risks
- **Worker-side render path (C.1/C.2):** the spec asserts pool series are "omitted without crashing" on workers but never pins *how* `janus_worker_pool:sched_stats/0` behaves when no pool runs (workers do serve :8090 per C.2). A gen_server call to a dead name exits; if /metrics or /stats/sched crashes on a worker, C.2/C.3 telemetry dies exactly during the dist storms it exists for. Highest-value pin.
- **B.1 flips the softness direction:** v2 residue = over-count → stranded capacity; the new take-vs-reconcile race = under-count → possible over-admission. Named and bounded, but the failure mode changed *shape*, not just size — runbook should say so.

## 3. Contradictions / stale claims
- C.1 "reading sched_stats/0 ONCE per scrape" vs C.2's dist local-read: that is two reads; F7's single-snapshot rule covers pool series only. Tighten.
- "A downward reconcile heal cannot false-arm" is overstated: the conjunct requires *tracked* jobs gone; the insert-failure class leaves reserve>0 with tracked empty, so a draining node can arm early (bounded — 30 s sticky, picks already stopped).
- No other stale text found.
