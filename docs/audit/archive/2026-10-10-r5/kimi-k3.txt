## 1. Verdict

**GO WITH FIXES** — all rev-5 folds are present and coherent; residuals are one stale seam reference, one probe-freshness contradiction, and two lifecycle gaps (purge-age vs long streams, probe rows in `rtt_map`).

## 2. Critical risks

- **Purge age (10 min) vs long-lived streams.** A legitimate active stream older than 10 min is still in the tracked set; the purge removes it and decrements once, then `done` decrements again → negative counter (tick-healed) **and** a capacity slot freed while the job still runs → over-admission. Cowboy `idle_timeout=300s` bounds *idle* streams, not *active* ones. Purge must either skip entries still tracked as alive or the age must exceed a pinned max job duration.
- **Probe-source rows and selection.** `sched_rtt` rows carry `Source :: passive|probe`, but the snapshot builder's `rtt_map` assembly never says whether probe-source rows feed the `rtt_key` sort. "Cold-start fill" implies they should (else the fill is gauge-only decoration), but unpinned → implementer coin-flip. Pin it.

## 3. Contradictions or stale claims

- **A.3 stale seam**: "dispatched to the target worker via the affinity-pin path (Part 0.2 — the pin explains the capacity exemption)" — rev 5 deleted exactly this wording; the seam is `dispatch_probe/2`. Part 0.2/0.7 are correct; A.3 is the orphan.
- **Probe freshness double definition**: Part 0.2 states both "only when no passive sample in 5 min" AND target = "absent/expired RTT" (10-min TTL). A (worker,provider) with a 6–10-min-old passive row satisfies the first and fails the second; `sched_probe_skip_total{fresh}` vs target-eligibility is ambiguous in that window.
- **`sched_geo_disabled_total` "ONCE at boot"** vs locus async load failure "auto-off + counter" happening post-boot — increment-once-per-disable-event, not once-ever.
- Divergence/probe-cadence state "cleared on nodedown" (0.2/0.3) vs living in the pool gen_server — owner crash without nodedown also clears it; say so.
