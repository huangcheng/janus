**1. Verdict**
GO WITH FIXES — all rev-5 folds are present and coherent, but the probe/internal-job boundary at the TTFB health EWMA and the `rtt_ms` piggyback is unseamed, silently violating two pinned invariants.

**2. Critical risks**
- Probe contamination: probes "report like any job" and done/error gain `rtt_ms` "per completion", so probe completions reach the canonical PASSIVE writer — violating the single-writer pin and "probe samples never enter passive EWMA". Workers also have no pinned way to identify probes ("normal job shapes") to keep them out of their own EWMA.
- Health masking: nothing excludes internal/probe completions from the master TTFB EWMA; fast 1-token probes dilute a sick worker's EWMA and can suppress demotion (13.6 math assumes real-job TTFB only).

**3. Contradictions / stale claims**
- Part A.3 still says probes go "via the affinity-pin path … the pin explains the capacity exemption" — exactly the wording rev 5 deleted; pinned picks *do* increment the counter, so the explanation is wrong (the exemption is structural, `dispatch_probe/2`).
- `rtt_ms_other` is absent from the `/stats/sched` exact-key list; its presence (always vs overflow-only) is unpinned while gates assert exact keys.

**4. Missing first-ship requirements**
- Internal-job exclusion pins at three seams (master rtt ingest, master TTFB feed, worker EWMA); `internal` must ride the job map to the worker.
- `geo_disabled_total` semantics for a post-boot async locus load failure, and selection state until the async load completes.

**5. Architecture/design notes**
- Exclude-self 3×median structurally guarantees the min-EWMA survivor (threshold ≥ 3m > m), so never-shed-all is provably inert — correct as a defensive guard; the rev-5 rewording holds.
- Corrective-write clamping + 30 s reconcile is acceptable; dead-node zero orphan rows linger harmlessly.
- Snapshot-drained pin fans out while live-drained pin falls back locally — asymmetry bounded by ≤ 250 ms snapshot staleness, fine.
- Default subdivision→tag rules are implementer-defined; gates don't depend on them (GEO_TEST_HOSTS), acceptable.
