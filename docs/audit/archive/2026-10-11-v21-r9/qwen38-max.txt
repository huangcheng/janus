**2. Critical risks**
- **B.2 sort-key table access unpinned.** `pick/1` is caller-side; the live `sched_reserve` read is pinned public + `read_concurrency`, but the live read of the `sched_rtt` `{Node,Provider}` Ewma cell is not. If `sched_rtt` stays gen_server-private, every RTT-knob-ON pick badargs on the hot path — violating "request never fails because of bookkeeping."
- **13.7 UNARY phase has no dispatch barrier.** "kill w1 before first byte" is timing-hoped, not gated: if the kill lands pre-dispatch the job goes to w2/local and the `unary_fallback ≥ 1` assert flakes. Pin: poll `dispatch_worker{w1,_}` delta ≥ 1 before `docker kill`.
- **Nodedown delete-after-take window.** Between the per-key take loop and `ets:delete(sched_reserve, Node)`, a re-hello'd pick's fresh increment is wiped → under-admit-by-1 until that job completes. Same accepted class as take-vs-reconcile, but *unnamed*, while the spec claims all softness is named.
