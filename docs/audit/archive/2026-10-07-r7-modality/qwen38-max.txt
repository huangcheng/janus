**1. Verdict:** GO WITH FIXES — seven rounds have hardened nearly everything, but the usage-row model still doesn't coherently cover non-video terminal status, and a runtime-leader mechanism is assumed yet never tasked.

**2. Critical risks**
- **Terminal-row home gap:** M1.1 says attempt rows never carry `status`, yet the TTS contract requires a row recording "chars + final status" and sync-abort needs a writer-owned `truncated` row. Terminal-row machinery is only specified for jvid video rows — images/TTS/ASR have no home for `status`.
- **Cross-node insert-once:** "guarded by the video_jobs.status transition" needs a dialect-portable CAS (`UPDATE ... WHERE status=<non-terminal>` + affected-rows check on epgsql *and* esqlite); two nodes polling the same jvid can otherwise both finalize. Unspecified.
- **Leader assumption:** M3.1b single-writer secret bootstrap and M3.3b nightly sweep both require a runtime leader; no task creates the designation/election mechanism.
- **Poll fallback undefined** when the provider is transport-unreachable but a local terminal row exists (persisted-result whole point partially wasted).
- Pre-accept 270s budget shared with failover retries can exhaust before the final attempt; ASR cap-reject drain has no budget (slow-upload occupancy).

- T.1 lumps the mock "per phase", contradicting R5's per-ship-unit mock task fix.
