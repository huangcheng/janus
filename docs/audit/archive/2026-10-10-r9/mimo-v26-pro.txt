## 3. Residuals

**R1 — unpinned drain-skip has no metric label (real gap).** Part C: "drain-skips of unpinned candidates label the exhaustion too" — but `dispatch_local_total` enumerates only `empty_pool|capacity|drained_pin|send_fail|ack_miss`, and Part 0.6 reserves `drained_pin` for the *pinned* case. Under the first-non-ok rule, an unpinned drain-skip ahead of a capacity refusal selects a label the vocabulary cannot express. Add a `drained` value or pin the mapping.

**R2 — probe abort leaves a tracked entry with undefined disposal (real gap).** Part 0.2: abort ⇒ "clear the in-flight entry" — nothing about the `{track,...}` entry. It then ages out at the 10-min purge; Internal skips the *decrement* but the spec doesn't say the purge skips `sched_reserve_purged_total` for Internal, and the abort doesn't cast `{release, JobRef}`. Result: every forced-probe retry (13.4b budgets 100/hour) inflates `reserve_purged_total`. Either abort releases (idempotent, decrement-neutral) or purge must be specified counter-neutral *and* counter-silent for Internal.

**R3 — E.6(c) keeps the compare semantics rev 9 deleted.** "never clobbers a newer `SampledAtMono` (write-time latest-wins)" is the compare-before-overwrite wording A.3 now calls vacuous. Under last-arrival-wins the assertion is just "probe fill writes only when no unexpired row exists". Reword; as written it re-teaches the dead rule.

**R4 — probe in-flight map key story mismatch (minor).** Part 0.2: `{Node => JobRef}`; Part 0.11: "the in-flight map clears by JobRef". Either key by JobRef or state the reverse scan.

**R5 — probe spend source unnamed (minor).** Probes "spend under the SEPARATE additive budget" while `janus_usage` filters internal jobs at insert. If the entitlement-probe budget meters usage rows, probes are free and the "separate budget" is unimplementable as written. Name the meter (dedicated counter vs usage-derived).
