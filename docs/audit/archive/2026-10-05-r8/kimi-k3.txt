## 2. Critical risks

- **SSE state machine** is the densest risk: text↔thinking block transitions, finish→usage→terminator ordering, zero-content exception. The eunit-first rule covers it, but only if fixtures include the transition cases, not just happy paths.
- **include_usage retry**: "before any 200" must be decided from gun's first response message; a provider that 200s then errors mid-stream must not trigger re-encode. Retry path shares counters/track — a second 400 must surface once.
- **gun:cancel vs mailbox**: cancel is idempotent, but already-delivered `gun_data` messages can arrive after cancel; drain must treat post-terminal events as no-ops (terminal_sent guards this — keep it airtight).
- **Inner-flag restore**: `janus_stats_inner` put/erase in `after` is correct only if the inner `do_proxy` crash still runs the restore; a missed restore silently mis-counts the outer call.
- **Cowboy 300s inactivity**: OpenAI-compatible providers that go silent during long generation (no ping, unlike Anthropic) will trip idle_timeout → client sees truncated stream recorded 502. Accepted risk (§10 closed), but operators should know the symptom.
- **_build volume ABI**: stale-beam failure mode is silent wrong-test-results, not a clean error.

## 5. Architecture notes

Handler-owned `#sse_st{}` with single leftover owner, immediate frame writes, and process-dict flags confined to the Cowboy process are the right shapes. Atomics in persistent_term created in `janus_http_app` before listeners avoids start-order races. The fragile seams are inner/outer flag discipline and the translate-path's exemption from `collect_drain` — both are correctly specified but deserve the most review attention. Paired dashboard PRs for TEST-FLOWS and deploy_prod.sh are correctly scoped.
