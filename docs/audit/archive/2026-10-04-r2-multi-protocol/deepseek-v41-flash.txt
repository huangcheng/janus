1. **Verdict: GO WITH FIXES** 鈥?architecture is sound, but the plan under-specifies field-level translation, error contracts, and streaming failure semantics enough to ship bugs.

2. **Critical risks**
- **Protocol match is 1:1 by assumption.** A provider may expose both `/chat/completions` and `/responses`; matching only on `provider.protocol` forces unnecessary translation and blocks native streaming.
- **SSE passthrough:** status is already 200 when a mid-stream upstream error arrives, so "reuse cooldown rules on terminal status" is unimplementable as written. No backpressure/flow-control, `[DONE]` vs `message_stop`, or disconnect-cancel spec.
- **Tool fidelity:** plan ignores `tool_choice`, parallel tool calls, `tool_calls[].id` 鈫?`tool_use.id` stability, `finish_reason: tool_calls` 鈫?`stop_reason: tool_use`, `is_error` on `tool_result`, and string-vs-block `content`. Silent corruption risk.
- **Auth:** `x-api-key` must be stripped before upstream forwarding; Anthropic adapter injects provider key. "Header filtering" is too vague 鈥?a leak sends client key to provider.
- **Auto-router:** internal chat pivot means messages/responses clients get double translation after tier pick; if the picked provider isn't the client protocol, streaming is refused 鈥?must be surfaced, not silent.
- **Anthropic required fields** (`max_tokens`, top-level `system`, `stop_sequences`) aren't mapped at all.
