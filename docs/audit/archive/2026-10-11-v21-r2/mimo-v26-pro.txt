**1. Verdict: GO WITH FIXES** — all 18 round-1 folds are present and coherent, but two stale claims survived the fold pass (schema_version, rtt_ms_avg definition) and one aggregation step is underspecified.

**2. Critical risks**
- Part I checklist still says "JSON schema_version 2", directly contradicting the fold "schema_version stays 1" (and 0.3/A.3). An implementer ticking Part I would bump it and break pinned consumers.
- B.1 nodedown "ets:take the node's tracked rows": `sched_tracked` is keyed by JobRef, so there is no take-by-node; it needs select-then-take per key (racing caller releases) or a per-node index. **3. Contradictions or stale claims**

- A.3 needs a node-level value, but B.2 stores EWMA per {Node,Provider}. "One canonical number everywhere" holds per provider; the per-node aggregation across providers (mean of EWMA columns? weighted?) is undefined and nondeterministic if done by table fold. Pin it (e.g., arithmetic mean of the node's unexpired EWMA cells).
