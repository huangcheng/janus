**1. Verdict:** GO WITH FIXES — coherent on caller-side bookkeeping, incremental EWMA, drain re-arming, and chaos bounds, but carries two internal contradictions and a few unresolved state-location questions that must be pinned before ship.

**2. Critical risks**
- **Pre-byte predicate owner (Part E).** "no chunk forwarded AND no done/error for the JobRef" implies per-JobRef state on the master session; the spec doesn't say where it lives or how it's cleared on the release-before-LocalFun step. Adopt must drop it too.
- **Probe-loss drain-idle drag.** A.2 explicitly lets a LOST probe delay sticky up to 10 min (purge TTL). A draining node can sit non-sticky for ten minutes on one dead probe; either document loudly or arm by non-Internal-empty only.
- **B.1 race claim not covered by cited eunit.** Counter-bump-then-insert between gen_server `pick/1` return and caller write isn't what the v2 concurrent capacity-1 fixture proves (concurrent-pick vs release isn't proved). Add one capacity-N race fixture.

- **rtt gauge canonical EWMA**: B.2 says one canonical number everywhere; C.1 says the gauge follows a "64-most-recent-unexpired window". Same metric, same renderer — reconcile.
- C.2 says "only master's counters surface in sched_stats/0", but workers also serve :8090 /stats/sched; per-node render under-reports on worker nodes. Either master-merge or rename to `*_local`.
