**2. Critical risks**
- **C.2 render path is unimplementable as written if the pool is master-only.** "Every node renders its own `janus_sched_dist_*` on its own /stats/sched" plus "Exposed through `sched_stats/0`" assumes `janus_worker_pool` (and an admin :8090 listener) runs on workers. If the pool is master-only, workers have no `sched_stats/0` and no surface — and dist storms are exactly when the worker side matters. Pin: pool-on-workers, or a separate `janus_dist_monitor:stats/0` folded into the local admin render.
- **Drain-idle false-arm race.** Arming reads `reserve == 0` while picks bump reserve caller-side; a pick landing between the emptiness check and the arm (or after) arms sticky around an in-flight job. Same race existed in v2 off `member.inflight` (not a regression), but the spec pins only the reconcile case. Draining-node selectability must be pinned.
