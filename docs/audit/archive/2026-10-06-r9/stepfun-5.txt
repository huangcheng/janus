**2. Critical risks**

- **Cowboy idle vs paused SSE.** 300s inactivity (not wall clock) means one Anthropic extended-thinking pause >300s mid-stream becomes a post-header 502. Closed decision; TF-6.8 fixtures should avoid long-thinking models so the gate doesn't flake.
- **janus-auto inner flags.** `janus_stats_inner` only holds if inner judge/target `do_proxy` runs *in the Cowboy handler process*. A spawning/parallel adjudicator leaks inner 5xx into outer `failed` and breaks the TF E2E ("inner judge 5xx with successful fallback must not increment failed").
